Privacy policy

Camp Gan Israel Cherry Hill respects your privacy. We use personal information only in the ways described here.

Information we collect

We collect personal information only when you choose to give it to us, for example when you fill out a camp form (hot lunch, birthday, trip permission or staff application), email us or call the office. This can include names, phone numbers, email addresses, your child's name and division, and the details the form asks for.

Camp registration and payments are handled through UltraCamp, which has its own privacy policy. Our website forms never ask for credit card numbers.

How we use it

  • To provide the services you request, such as hot lunch orders and trip sign-ups
  • To send camp updates, reminders and notices
  • To confirm registrations, forms and donations
  • To answer your questions

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, swap or otherwise share our mailing lists or your personal information with outside organizations. We share information only with your permission, when required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our campers, staff and camp. Information may be shared with Chabad Lubavitch of Camden and Burlington Counties, our parent organization.

Camp photos

We post camp photos on this website, Facebook and Instagram. We don't post campers' last names. If you'd prefer that your child's photo not be posted, or want a photo taken down, email info@welovecgi.com.

Website basics

Like most websites, our host may record basic technical information such as browser type and IP address to keep the site running and secure. We use it only in aggregate. This site links to outside websites, such as UltraCamp, Facebook and Instagram, and we aren't responsible for their privacy practices.

Emails

We won't add you to a mailing list without your request or agreement, and you can ask to be removed at any time.

Questions

Email info@welovecgi.com or call 856-438-0607. Camp Gan Israel Cherry Hill is an affiliate of Chabad Lubavitch of Camden and Burlington Counties, Medford, NJ.
