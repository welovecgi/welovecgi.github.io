Parent Corner

Lice prevention policy

Head lice are a nuisance, not a health threat. Working together, camp and families keep outbreaks away.

Screenings

All campers are checked for lice and nits (eggs) during the first few days of camp and from time to time all summer, with extra checks when needed. If a camper has lice or nits, camp follows the procedures below right away.

How parents can help

  1. Check your child's head regularly for lice or nits.
  2. If you find lice, treat right away and let camp know.
  3. Keep long hair tied back.
  4. Keep bedding, car seats, couches and carpets clean.

If lice are found at camp

  1. Campers with live lice or untreated nits are sent home. They may come back 24 hours after an insecticide treatment. Herbal or botanical treatments don't count.
  2. After treatment, camp has a no-nit policy. Campers with treated nits may return only with a note from a dermatologist confirming the nits are dead.
