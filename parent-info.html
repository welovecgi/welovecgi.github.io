Frequently asked questions

How does my camper find a lost item?

Label everything with your camper's name and group. Lost & Found is checked at the office every day. If something specific is missing, call or email the office.

What if my child doesn't want to join an activity?

No camper is forced to join. We encourage everyone to try each activity so they get the most out of camp.

Can my camper bring a special toy or device?

No toys or electronics, please. Camp provides everything your child needs.

How often do campers drink water?

Kellman has water fountains and bottle-filling stations throughout the building, and every field has water coolers with cups.

Do we have to tip our child's counselor?

Tips are appreciated but never required. A common guideline is $20 – $25 for junior counselors and $30 – $40 for counselors. Staff payment details are shared with families near the end of camp.

How religious is Camp Gan Israel?

Campers come from every Jewish background. We don't focus on individual observance. We share a joyful Jewish spirit through songs, projects and activities.

How can I get more involved?

We love having "bunk moms" and parent helpers. Let the Director know you're interested.
