Parent Corner

Swimming

Campers swim at Willowdale Swim Club, with a big pool, a shallow area for younger swimmers, a separate baby pool and lots of shade.

What to send on swim days

Pack a bag labeled with your child's name:

  • Towel
  • Swimsuit
  • Water shoes (optional)
  • Sunscreen for reapplying

Sunscreen

Please put sunscreen on your child at home every morning before camp.

Swim days

Swim days are on your division's calendar. Kiddy Camp swims on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays, and toddlers have water play at camp on those days. Swim days can change because of weather, so check the calendar for updates.

Toddler water play

Toddlers stay at camp for water play. Send a swim diaper, bathing suit and towel.
