Camp hours & pick-up

All drop-off and pick-up is at Kellman Brown Academy, 1007 Laurel Oak Road, Voorhees, NJ 08043.

West Side busLeaves 8:40 AM

Drop-off

  • Before-care campers can arrive from 8:00 AM. Park and ring the front doorbell.
  • Regular drop-off starts at 8:50 AM. Pull into the carpool loop at the side entrance and help your child out of the car. A staff member will greet them and walk them in.
  • If you park, use only marked spots and walk your child to the side entrance.
  • Arriving after 9:15 AM? Park, walk your child to the front door and ring the bell.
  • Parents may not enter the building unless authorized.

Pick-up

  • Pick-up starts at 3:15 PM, and camp ends at 3:30 PM.
  • Use the same side-entrance carpool loop. Stay in your car and staff will bring your child to you.
  • After-care campers can stay until 5:30 PM. Park and ring the front doorbell.

Someone else picking up?

Add them as an authorized pick-up in your UltraCamp account, or let the camp office know by phone or email. Changes must reach the Camp Director by 12:00 PM that day. Camp can't accept pick-up instructions from campers.
