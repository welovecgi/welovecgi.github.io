Staff job descriptions

Our mission: a safe, fun and uplifting summer where children grow through joy, friendship and a deeper love for Judaism.

Sign your staff agreement

Counselors Grades 11 & up

Counselors are the campers' main caregivers, responsible for supervision, engagement, safety and a structured, positive camp day.

Responsibilities

  • Be fully responsible for your campers at all times, including trips and transitions.
  • Make sure campers take part and enjoy programs. Lead activities with energy.
  • Help with sports, art, music, trips and special events.
  • Support daily routines, transitions, teamwork and good behavior.
  • Model and inspire love of Torah, mitzvot and Jewish pride.
  • Follow all safety procedures and report concerns right away.

Every day

  • Arrive on time and prepared.
  • Stay with your group and keep the bunk clean.
  • Follow the staff schedule and announcements.
  • Bring patience, energy and positivity.

Never

  • Use your phone during camp hours.
  • Leave campers unattended.
  • Use inappropriate language or conversations around campers.
  • Have physical contact beyond appropriate, safe interactions.
  • Ignore instructions from leadership.

Watch for campers' emotional and physical needs, report every injury or incident right away, and follow emergency procedures. Communicate respectfully, praise often and correct privately, keep instructions simple, and always have a backup plan.

Junior Counselors Grade 10

Junior Counselors are real staff members with real responsibility. They support the counselor by helping manage campers, leading activities and keeping the day running smoothly.

Responsibilities

  • Help keep order and support transitions.
  • Lead games, help with activities and support individual campers.
  • Redirect behavior when appropriate, and report serious issues to the counselor.
  • Bring positive energy at all times.

Every day

  • Sign in and out at the front desk.
  • Put your phone in your assigned slot on arrival.
  • Arrive on time and stay with your bunk.
  • Keep your walkie-talkie on.

Never

  • Use your phone during camp hours.
  • Have adult conversations around campers.
  • Leave your group without permission.
  • Go into bathrooms with campers. Stand outside only.
  • Have physical contact beyond high fives.

Need help?

Talk to your Counselor or Head Counselor first, then Rabbi Yitzchok or Baily.

CITs Grades 8 & 9

CITs support counselors, observe and help with daily camp life while learning how camp runs. They aren't responsible for discipline or leadership, but their attitude and effort make a real difference.

Responsibilities

  • Be a positive presence and bring energy.
  • Help counselors with setup, cleanup and activities.
  • Help campers who need extra attention, with counselor supervision.
  • Watch how counselors lead, and ask questions.

Every day

  • Sign in and out at the front desk.
  • Put your phone in your assigned slot.
  • Arrive on time and stay with your bunk.
  • Be kind, respectful and enthusiastic.

Never

  • Use your phone during camp hours.
  • Discipline campers. Refer concerns to a counselor.
  • Have inappropriate conversations around campers.
  • Have physical contact beyond high fives.
  • Go into bathrooms with campers. Wait outside.
  • Leave your group without permission.

Need help?

Talk to your counselor or Head Counselor, or Baily.

Every staff member must read their job description and sign the staff agreement. CITs read it together with their parents.
