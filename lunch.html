Nut-free and allergies

CGI is a peanut and tree nut-free camp. Please don't send any food with peanuts or tree nuts. Some campers are allergic to sesame, so please avoid sesame foods like tahini, hummus and sesame bagels when you can.

Snacks and water

Camp gives healthy snacks every day, including fresh fruit and vegetables. Send a labeled water bottle every day.
