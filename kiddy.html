Parent Corner

Kiddy Camp & toddlers

Everything you need to get your little one ready for camp.

Meet & Greet

Come meet the staff, see the Kiddy Camp classrooms and drop off your camper's belongings before the first day. A yellow school bus will be there so children can get used to the bus they'll ride to swim.

WhenSunday, 11 – 12
WhereKellman Brown

[To update: 2027 Meet & Greet date. Last summer it was Sunday, June 21.] Can't make it, or starting later in the summer? Send everything on your child's first day.

Divisions

  • Kiddy Camp (ages 3 – 5): must be fully potty trained.
  • Toddlers (under 3 by June 30): potty training not required. Toddlers have their own calendar.

What to send: Kiddy Camp

  • Rest mat and/or blanket
  • Complete change of clothes
  • Box of tissues
  • Box of wipes

What to send: Toddlers

  • 2 full changes of clothes
  • Diapers and wipes
  • Rest mat and/or blanket
  • Pack 'n Play (if needed)

Please label everything with your child's name.

Swimming: Kiddy Camp

Swimming at Willowdale Swim Club on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays. Send your child wearing their swimsuit, with a labeled towel, water shoes and a change of clothes.

Water play: Toddlers

Water play at camp on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays. Send a swim diaper, bathing suit and towel.

Sunscreen, lunch and water

Put sunscreen on before drop-off. You can also send labeled sunscreen for staff to reapply. See Lunch & snacks for lunch, and send a labeled water bottle every day.

Staying in touch

Call the office at 856-438-0607 during camp hours. Messages are checked every hour. Important updates go out by email, so keep your contact information current. Follow us on Facebook and Instagram.

Baily Kahan
Director, CGI Kiddy Camp
