About our camp

At Camp Gan Israel, campers grow with a deep pride in their Jewish heritage and a love for the Land of Israel. Every week has its own Jewish theme, brought to life through activities, stories, songs, games and contests, along with group discussions and fun educational programs.

Add in trips, swimming, sports, crafts and friendships that last all year, and it's a summer your child will never forget.
