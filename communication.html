Reaching camp

  • Call the camp office at 856-438-0607 during camp hours. Messages are checked every hour.
  • Email info@welovecgi.com.
  • To talk with your child's counselor, leave a message with the office and the counselor will call you back.
  • The directors are at the camp office during camp hours.

Pick-up changes

Any change to who's picking up must reach the Camp Director by 12:00 PM on the day of the change. You can also add authorized pick-ups in your UltraCamp account.

How we keep you posted

Important camp updates are sent by email, so please keep your contact information current in UltraCamp. Follow along on Facebook and Instagram @cgicherryhill, and see weekly photos once camp starts.
