Parent Corner

Everything parents need to know, in one place. Anything else? Email info@welovecgi.com or call 856-438-0607.

Parent Corner All FAQs
Camp hours, pick-up & drop-off

Camp runs 9:00 AM – 3:30 PM, with before-care from 8:00 AM and after-care until 5:30 PM. Drop-off starts at 8:50 AM and pick-up at 3:15 PM in the side-entrance carpool loop. Read more

Swimming

Campers swim at Willowdale Swim Club. On swim days, send a labeled bag with a towel, swimsuit, water shoes and sunscreen, and put sunscreen on at home. Read more

Lunch, snacks & food policy

Optional kosher hot lunch on Mondays and Tuesdays, pizza from camp on Wednesdays, and a dairy or pareve lunch from home on other days. Camp is peanut and tree nut-free. Read more

Trip & transportation

Juniors and Pioneers go on trips with T&L Transportation and wear their CGI T-shirt on trip days. A daily bus leaves the West Side of Cherry Hill at 8:40 AM and returns at 3:50 PM. Read more

Kiddy & toddler information

What to send, rest time, swim and water play days, and the Meet & Greet before camp starts. Read more

Communication

Call the camp office at 856-438-0607 or email info@welovecgi.com. Messages are checked every hour, and pick-up changes must reach the Camp Director by 12:00 PM. Read more

Frequently asked questions

Lost items, toys and devices, water, tipping, getting involved and more. Read more
