Pioneer trips

Our Pioneers take two special trips each summer. Every Pioneer family fills out each trip form, even if your child isn't going, so we know where your child will be.

Dates below are from Summer 2026 and will be updated for 2027. [To update: 2027 trip dates]

Six Flags adventure + overnight

Leave campTue, 10:00 AM
Optional pick-up8:00 PM at Six Flags
Back to campWed, for dismissal

Campers spend the day at Six Flags, then sleep in prefab tents at Nomoco Campground in Turkey Swamp Park. All meals are provided by camp. Last summer the trip was Tuesday–Wednesday, July 7–8.

Overnight policy: talk with your child to make sure they're comfortable staying overnight. If they're unsure, pick them up at 8:00 PM at Six Flags. Once the group leaves Six Flags for the campground, there's no pick-up option.

What to bring: sleeping bag, pillow, pajamas, camp shirt, change of clothes, swim gear and towel, toiletries, flashlight, bug spray, sunscreen, water bottle and spending money ($10 suggested).

Cost: $175 full overnight ($150 with a season pass), or $125 day trip and dinner ($100 with a season pass).

Club Getaway overnight

Leave campTue, 8:00 AM
ReturnWed, about 6 PM
Trip fee$175

Pioneers travel by chartered bus to Club Getaway in Kent, Connecticut, a full campground with lots of outdoor activities run by its trained staff. Campers sleep in air-conditioned cabins with bathrooms, showers and beds, with sheets, pillows and comforters provided. All meals are catered kosher. Last summer the trip was Tuesday–Wednesday, July 28–29, and the form was due July 21.

What to bring (in one labeled bag): swimsuit, two towels, sunscreen, camp T-shirt, pajamas, sneakers or closed-toe shoes, slippers or shower shoes, change of clothes, sweatshirt, toiletries, flashlight, refillable water bottle and up to $10 spending money.

Phones: electronics are only allowed on the bus and are collected on arrival. Cell phones aren't permitted. To reach your child, call camp at 856-438-0607. Campers can call home in the evening on a staff phone.
