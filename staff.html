Before you apply

  • Counselor positions are for staff who have finished 10th grade or older. Younger teens can join as Junior Counselors (grade 10) or CITs (grades 8 – 9).
  • You must attend staff orientation and training the week before camp, plus online pre-camp training.
  • We get more applications than positions, and choose staff based on qualifications and fit. Every complete application is reviewed, including reference checks.
