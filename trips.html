Parent Corner

Trips & transportation

Juniors and Pioneers go on trips all summer with T&L Transportation, and a daily bus runs from the West Side of Cherry Hill.

West Side bus

Congregation Sons of Israel, 720 Cooper Landing Rd., Cherry Hill, NJ 08002

Parent drop-off8:30 AM
Bus leaves8:40 AM
Bus returns3:50 PM

The bus leaves at 8:40 AM sharp and can't wait for latecomers.

Trip days

  • Juniors and Pioneers travel to trips on T&L Transportation buses.
  • Campers wear their CGI T-shirt on every trip day. Each camper gets a shirt on the first day of camp, and extra shirts are $15.
  • Trip days can change because of weather. Check your division's calendar.
  • Pioneers have special trips too. See Pioneer trips.

Bus rules

  • Seat belts are provided and must be worn the whole ride.
  • Campers stay seated for the entire ride.
  • A bus monitor takes attendance every day.
  • Kiddy Campers sit in the front 8 seats, and families sit together when possible.
  • Riding the bus is a privilege. Campers who don't behave safely may lose bus privileges.
